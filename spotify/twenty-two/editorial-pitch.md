For fans of Foo Fighters, Nirvana, and Pearl Jam.

## 🎤 WHY THIS SONG

"Twenty-Two" is the title track and the emotional pivot of the album. It's the moment the singer realizes his tour-bus life is over and the kid he used to be is gone. The lyric is conversational, the production is intimate (acoustic guitar + vocal + soft strings), and the vocal performance is the most revealing of the album. It's the track that captures the album's central question — *who am I without the band?* — and the answer it offers (the music is still there, the person is still there, you just have to remember where you started).

## 📝 BACKUP PITCHES (if first pitch rejected)

### Pitch 2 (Lead single): "Razor"

> Three chords, a kid's first guitar, his mother's living room. Razor is the opener of *Twenty-Two* — 138 BPM, E minor, fifty seconds of yelling because the song is so much better than the singer is. The whole album in one song. Raw, hungry, awake. For fans of Foo Fighters' "All My Life" energy and Nirvana's "In Utero" rawness.

### Pitch 3 (Most accessible): "Mama"

> The quietest track on *Twenty-Two* — five minutes and forty-nine seconds of folk for the mother who never said stop. The mother who came to every show. The mother who picked up the phone every time the tour van broke down at 4 AM. Set in F major, 80 BPM, acoustic guitar + vocal. For fans of early Death Cab and the Mountain Goats' "Goths" energy.

### Pitch 4 (Album closer): "Standing Still"

> Nine minutes and forty-two seconds of resolution. The album's final chorus arrives with the recurring G–C–D–Em motif played full-band with strings. The lyric is the moment after the album ends — looking back at all of it and being okay. The closer, the resolution, the album summarized in 9:42. For fans of Foo Fighters' "In Your Honor" acoustic-side energy.

## 📈 EXPECTED OUTCOMES

Industry data: Spotify accepts ~10% of editorial pitches. That's a 90% rejection rate. Rejection is normal.

**If accepted:**
- Track added to 1-3 Spotify editorial playlists
- Discovery boost for 4-8 weeks
- Potential 10,000-100,000 additional streams
- Often leads to algorithmic playlist inclusion (Discover Weekly, Release Radar)

**If rejected:**
- Album still releases normally
- Algorithmic playlists still work (Discover Weekly, Release Radar, Daily Mixes)
- Re-pitch for next release
- Editorial pitch rejections are not stored against future artists

**Realistic outcome for indie first album:** probably rejected. Focus on growing Spotify for Artists presence and pitching for the second album.
