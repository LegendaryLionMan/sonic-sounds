### Album story (short)

*Twenty-Two* is a coming-of-age grunge record that traces the arc of an entire adolescence — fifteen to twenty-two years old — in twelve short stories told in chronological order.

The album opens with the first guitar a kid ever owned. It closes with the moment after the album ends, looking back at all of it.

Pyro Altar is a four-piece from Pacifica, California — Cole Sterling (vocals, guitar), River Holloway (guitar), Mace Voss (bass), and Ash Renner (drums). They've played every dive bar, house show, and VFW hall between San Francisco and Los Angeles. *Twenty-Two* is their debut album.
