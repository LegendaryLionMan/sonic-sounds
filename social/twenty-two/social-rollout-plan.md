## 🎯 STRATEGY

Build anticipation, drive pre-saves, capture the release moment, sustain engagement into the post-release window.

**Three arcs:**
1. **Anticipation (T-60 to T-1):** create desire, capture pre-saves, build the email list
2. **Launch (T-0 to T+7):** maximize first-day streams, ride algorithmic momentum
3. **Sustain (T+7 to T+60):** keep the album alive, build for the next release

**Three platforms:**
- **Instagram** (primary) — visual storytelling, Reels for short music clips
- **TikTok** (discovery) — short-form vertical video, viral potential
- **YouTube** (depth) — longer videos, lyric videos, behind-the-scenes

**Secondary platforms (lower priority):**
- X (Twitter) — quick updates, conversation
- Facebook (low ROI for indie) — share once for completeness
- Threads — same as X

## 💰 PAID PROMOTION (Optional)

**Skip these for first release** — focus on organic. Only consider paid promotion if you have $200+ to spend AND a clear target:

| Platform | Cost | When | Targeting |
|---|---|---|---|
| Instagram Ads | $50-200 | T-7 to T+7 | Lookalike audiences, fans of similar artists |
| TikTok Promote | $50-100 | T-3 to T+3 | Snippet-based, algorithm-driven |
| Spotify Ad Studio | $250-500 | T+7 to T+30 | Listener profiles, genre targeting |
| SubmitHub | $1-5/submission | T-14 onwards | Playlist curators (low ROI at this stage) |

**Skip for release 1.** Build audience organically first. Use paid promotion for release 2, when you have a baseline to compare against.
