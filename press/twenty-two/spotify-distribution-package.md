## 💰 ROYALTY MATH

**Assumptions:**
- Album gets 100,000 streams in year 1 (optimistic for indie first release)
- Average payout per stream: $0.003 (Spotify)
- Total payout: $300

**RouteNote Free (15% royalty):**
- 100,000 streams × $0.003 = $300
- RouteNote takes 15% = $45
- Artist receives: $255

**RouteNote Premium ($30 one-time, 100% royalty):**
- 100,000 streams × $0.003 = $300
- RouteNote takes 0% = $0
- Artist receives: $300
- Net: $300 - $30 (premium cost) = $270

**Break-even:** RouteNote Premium only worth it if streams > $200/yr (200,000 × $0.003 = $600 wholesale, 15% = $90 saved, exceeds $30 cost).

**Conclusion:** RouteNote Free is correct for expected first-year income. Upgrade to Premium when individual releases consistently exceed $200/yr.

## 📞 TROUBLESHOOTING

**Q: RouteNote rejects the upload.**
A: Check (1) bitrate ≥ 128 kbps, (2) cover art ≥ 3000x3000, (3) no metadata fields empty, (4) all 12 tracks present. Re-export from sources if needed.

**Q: Tracks are live but not showing in Spotify for Artists.**
A: Wait 24-48 hours. Then check the artist name spelling matches Spotify for Artists data exactly. If still missing, contact RouteNote support.

**Q: Editorial pitch was rejected.**
A: Not all pitches are accepted. Spotify selects ~10% of pitch submissions. Submit again for next release — past rejections don't disqualify future submissions.

**Q: The album is live but royalty payments are missing.**
A: Minimum payout threshold is $10 for RouteNote. Income accrues until threshold is met. Check payout schedule in RouteNote dashboard.

**Q: I want to remove the album.**
A: RouteNote allows takedown at any time. Track pages disappear within 24-48 hours. Already-paid royalties are not refunded.
