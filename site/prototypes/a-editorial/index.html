Panel II

Twenty-one questions.

Not a form — the first chapter of an editor's interview. Eight are mandatory; the rest have defaults ready if you skip them.

  1. What is this album about? Not genre, not vibe — the emotional or narrative heart.
  2. What scope? Single / EP / album / double-feature / concept piece.
  3. What genre family at a minimum?
  4. One to three reference artists whose sound you want to channel.
  5. Vocal approach — solo / duet / choir / spoken word / instrumental.
  6. Language(s) for lyrics.
  7. Runtime target per track — radio / standard / extended / immersive.
  8. Artist stage name.
  9. Working album title.
  10. Rough tracklist — titles or themes, your call.

…plus eleven more. Most have sensible defaults so you can skip them.
