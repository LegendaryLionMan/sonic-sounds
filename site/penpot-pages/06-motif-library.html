THE HALF-LIGHT MOTIF · USED CONSISTENTLY ACROSS STUDIO AND ALBUM

Why this motif

The motif is a half-lit window — the same one as Half-Light Hours. Consistency: the studio and the album feel like one artifact. Recall: anyone who's seen Half-Light Hours recognizes the studio. Proven: the motif already worked for a release, it's not invented for this site.

Three variants

SMALL · 96×72

Used in masthead corner as a quiet brand mark. 1px rule, half-light terracotta + highlight quadrants. Subtle presence, not loud.

MEDIUM · 192×144

Used in section headers and as a visual rhythm device between major content blocks. Carries the half-light metaphor at 1/4 page scale.

LARGE · 320×240

Used in footer as the closing image. Pairs with the studio name in display serif, half-italicized, against the warm paper background.
