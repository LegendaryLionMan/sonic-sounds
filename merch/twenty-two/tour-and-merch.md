## 📦 MERCH SUPPLY CHAIN

### Pre-tour production (8 weeks before launch)

1. **Finalize designs** (DONE — see `merch-01-tshirt-window-lineart.jpg`, `merch-02-vinyl-inner-sleeve.jpg`)
2. **Source suppliers** — Printful, Printify, or local screen printer
3. **Order mockups** (1 of each SKU, $50-100 total)
4. **Approve and bulk order** (4-6 weeks lead time)
5. **Inventory at home base** (need 200-300 sq ft of storage)

### Per-tour logistics

- **Day of show:** truck drives merch to venue, set up 2 tables, 1-2 merch sellers
- **Per-show merch target:** $3,705 average (see above)
- **Inventory restock:** shipped to home base weekly
- **Reconciliation:** end-of-night cash + card count

### Online sales (post-tour)

- Continue selling online via Shopify or Bandcamp
- Bandcamp takes 10-15% (less than Shopify's 2.9% + 30¢ but more indie-friendly)
- Net online margin: ~80% of gross
