Close-ups on fingers, fretboard, the song's lyric appearing as old pencil-marker on the wall behind him. End of video: the camera pulls back to reveal the empty garage and the guitar placed back on its stand.

**Storyboard:** 6 frames (see `storyboard-01-...-razor.jpg` ... `storyboard-06-...-razor.jpg`)

## 🎬 VIDEO 2: "Twenty-Two" (Title Track / Emotional Center)

**Treatment:** 4-minute narrative video. A 22-year-old wakes up in a hotel room the morning after a tour ends. We follow him through the day — packing, calling his mother, driving home, walking into his childhood bedroom, picking up the first guitar. Intercut with flashbacks of the tour (concerts, the van, the dive bar). End: he sits on the bed with the guitar, plays the opening of the album, smiles.

**Storyboard:** 6 frames (see `storyboard-07-...-twenty-two.jpg` ... `storyboard-12-...-twenty-two.jpg`)

**Why:** Title track deserves narrative scope. Coming-home story arc, emotional payoff.

**Production:** 3-4 locations (hotel, car, childhood home, garage), 1 actor, 1-2 days. Total cost: $0-1,000 (self-shot).

## 🎬 VIDEO 3: "Mama" (Tender / Quiet)

**Treatment:** 60-second performance video. Single performer (Cole) in a sunlit kitchen, singing the song directly to a phone camera propped on the table. The camera doesn't move. The mother is implied but never shown. End: he stops singing, says "Happy Mother's Day, mom" quietly, and the video cuts to black.

**Storyboard:** 1 frame (this is a single-shot performance, storyboard unnecessary)

**Why:** Lowest budget of the five. Maximum emotional impact. The "single-take" feel is the point.

**Production:** 1 location, 1 actor, 1 phone, 1 take. Total cost: $0.

## 🎬 VIDEO 4: "Freeway" (Tour Video / Pulse)

**Treatment:** 3-minute montage video. Dashcam footage of driving on the I-5. Intercut with performance shots of the band in a basement rehearsal space. End: the band drives away, the camera catches them in the rearview mirror, the song fades.

**Storyboard:** 1 frame (montage, storyboard the mood)

**Why:** Visceral, kinetic, tour-focused. Captures the energy of the song.

**Production:** 1 drive day, 1 rehearsal day, 1 camera. Total cost: $0-200.

## 🎬 VIDEO 5: "Standing Still" (Album Closer / Resolution)

**Treatment:** 5-minute cinematic video. Slow zoom into a single photograph — childhood bedroom of a 22-year-old. The photograph fades to a 360-degree slow pan of the actual room. The camera continues panning, revealing the same room 7 years later (now a tour-bus bunk). The pan continues, revealing the same room 14 years later (now a house, a kid, a guitar on the wall). Cut to: the band playing the song live on a stage, the camera at the back of the venue, the audience silhouetted. End: lights down, applause.

**Storyboard:** 1 frame (cinematic, slow, won't storyboard)

**Why:** Album closing track deserves a video that feels like an ending. The visual loop (past → present → future) mirrors the song's structure.

**Production:** 3 locations, 1 day each, 1 camera. Total cost: $0-500.
